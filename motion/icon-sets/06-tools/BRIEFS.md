# Twenty tools and controls — design briefs

This batch continues the existing original rounded family. Use a 32-unit grid, three native Outline widths, a related Solid master, 18% Duotone, and shared color tokens. Review at 24, 32, and 48 px, in Light and Strong motion. These are same-state acknowledgements or feature demonstrations. They do not report actual identity, connection, charge, printing, payment, or data state. Product state stays in the product.

All twenty share the single gallery's full-card hover loop, keyboard focus loop, one-shot touch preview, and reduced-motion rest. Native geometry and animation remain editable. Exact timings live in `definitions.mjs`; this table records the design decision before production.

| Icon | Selected action | Simpler alternative considered | Light / Strong and protected geometry |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| User | The head inclines over still shoulders. | A whole-avatar pulse hides its anatomy. | A short head shift versus a wider incline; protect the neck gap and circular head. |
| Users | The second member steps forward beside a fixed foreground profile. | Equal motion on both figures makes hierarchy unclear. | Increase the rear member's rise; keep the foreground still and heads separated. |
| Key | The complete key turns at its bow. | Loose teeth that wiggle break the rigid tool. | Wider Strong angle; bow, stem, and teeth remain one assembly. |
| Scan | A bar scans inside fixed brackets. | Drawing the brackets adds no scan mechanism. | Increase the vertical scan range; preserve bracket corners and line clearance. |
| Battery | The charge region expands within a fixed cell. | Blinking the shell resembles a warning. | Increase filled travel; keep a visible gap to the Solid frame and terminal. |
| Plug | The plug seats toward its cable attachment and releases. | A floating unplugged cable creates a broken joint. | Greater Strong seating travel; cable meets the moving body and retains its fixed lower end. |
| Bluetooth | The signal facets open about a fixed spine. | Extra radiating rings duplicate Volume. | Increase horizontal facet reach; keep its meaningful angular rune and rounded joins. |
| Monitor | The screen tilts at its stand. | Moving the whole monitor removes the reference. | Wider Strong angle; shared stand attachment stays fixed. |
| Smartphone | A tile scrolls within the screen. | A whole-device vibration could read as an alert. | Increase content travel; casing and speaker stay fixed, with content contained. |
| Laptop | The display folds toward its lower hinge. | A perspective spin would leave the flat family. | Greater Strong fold in the frontal drawing; base and lower hinge stay fixed. |
| Printer | Paper advances from its output slot. | A generic check would imply printing had succeeded. | Increase exposed sheet length; top of sheet, housing, and input paper stay fixed. |
| Document | Two content lines compose in order. | Tracing the outer page is not a writing action. | Increase the line extension; retain the left margin, folded corner, and content gaps. |
| Clipboard | The clip presses at its attachment. | Moving all contents duplicates Document. | Increase clip closure; board stays still and the clip keeps an attachment at its sides. |
| Book | The right leaf turns toward its spine. | Scaling both leaves obscures the page-turn mechanism. | Increase the fold; left leaf and spine stay fixed, Solid gutter stays open. |
| Credit card | The card makes one straight swipe. | A confirmation tick would falsely imply payment. | Increase lateral travel; the complete card and its marks stay rigid. |
| Wallet | The clasped flap opens from its right edge. | Moving the entire wallet does not identify its closure. | Increase flap reach; keep the right edge fixed and clasp inside the flap. |
| Gift | The lid and bow rise while the box base stays fixed. | Separate lid travel exposes three loose round line ends. | Retain the full Strong lift; box sides, ribbon, and filled upper boundary follow the lid underside with the same driver. |
| Flag | One cloth wave travels toward its free edge. | A pole rotation would move the attachment. | Increase cloth displacement; pole and hoist remain fixed, top and bottom share the wave. |
| Chart | Three bars grow in order from one baseline. | Pulsing the whole chart obscures relative values. | Increase each bar's useful height change; keep widths, gaps, and baseline fixed. |
| Filter | Lower tiers narrow in order under the fixed top line. | Sliding everything sideways resembles a menu gesture. | Increase narrowing; keep round terminals, centered tiers, and a brief stagger. |

Start visual studies with User, Printer, and Gift: anatomy, a feeding mechanism, and a joined moving assembly. Then review all twenty together through their rest, active poses, Strong peaks, and returns. Keep new findings with evidence in the shared learnings file; do not record unreviewed results as user acceptance.
