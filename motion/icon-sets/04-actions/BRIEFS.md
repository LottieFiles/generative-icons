# Ten actions — production briefs

All ten are original 32-unit vectors for product controls at 24/32/48 px. Match the existing eight icons: gently curved corners, round caps and joins, three native outline weights (1.5/2/2.5), an optical Solid master, and an 18% Duotone interior. Transparent exports use shared theme slots. Light and Strong retain identical state drawings. Native Engine MCP owns the export; no runtime substitute artwork.

The gallery loops complete demonstrations while hovered or keyboard focused. Touch previews once. In a product, acknowledgement gestures play once; Menu keeps the requested state until its separate reverse action is called. Reduced motion shows the current state. Coalesce retriggers; changing profile cancels old completions and selects matching downloads.

| Icon | Selected action and purpose | Alternative rejected | Parts, fixed points, and protected space | Light / Strong poses and range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Download | The arrow descends toward a receiving tray, then returns; acknowledges the download control without claiming transfer success. | A falling dot would lose the recognizable arrow. | Rigid shaft/head; stationary tray. Keep at least 2 units of painted gap at maximum approach. | Rest → descend → brief receiving hold → return. 1.3 / 3.2 units of arrow travel. |
| Upload | The arrow lifts away from its tray, then returns; previews outward transfer. | Reusing Download's downward motion would reverse meaning. | One rigid arrow above a stationary tray; protect the upper canvas margin. | Rest → lift → hold → return. 1.2 / 3.0 units upward. |
| Copy | The front sheet separates diagonally from its source and returns. | A whole-icon pulse would not demonstrate duplication. | Fixed exposed back-sheet edges; a rigid rounded front sheet. Do not show hidden back edges through the front. | Rest → separate → hold → align. (0.8,0.7) / (2.2,1.5) units. |
| Trash | The lid opens about its right contact, then closes. This is a control demonstration, not a claim that data was deleted. | Shrinking the bin would hide the control and imply a destructive result. | Rigid lid and attached handle; body responds only to closing contact. Rounded tapered body with one interior slot. | Closed → open → hold → close → settle. 5.5 / 14 degrees; Strong has a small body response after contact. |
| Edit | A rigid pencil lowers toward the writing rule, writes to the right, lifts, then returns. | Tracing a pencil outline would show construction instead of editing. | Pencil tip is the local pivot. The rule is stationary. Protect the top and right margins during travel. | Raised → contact → write → lift → return. 1.2 / 3.4 units of writing travel; Strong has a longer readable contact. |
| Send | The plane advances along its pointing direction and returns on a short recovery arc. | A full exit and fade-in would break the reusable silhouette and loop. | Rigid plane and fold, both moving together. No trail or particles. | Rest → prepare → advance → hold → return. 0.8 / 2.2 units diagonally; preparation is only 0.15 / 0.4 units. |
| Bookmark | The lower ribbon gathers upward and releases while the top attachment stays fixed. This acknowledges the save control; it does not toggle saved state. | Filling the ribbon as a temporary selection would imply an unrequested state change. | Fixed rounded top, flexible lower ribbon, open central notch. Solid receives its own rounded silhouette. | Rest → gather → hold → release. Lower-edge travel 1.2 / 3.5 units; Strong adds a small lateral fold. |
| Refresh | Two attached arrow assemblies make one clockwise turn. This acknowledges the refresh control; actual loading requires product state. | Reversing a short turn would suggest undo rather than refresh. | Each arrow remains rigid; Strong allows the two assemblies to open apart by 1.8 units while turning. | Rest → clockwise turn → decelerate into matching rest. Both turn 360 degrees; Strong adds radial separation, not only duration. |
| Eye | The eyelids close and reopen; a short visibility-control acknowledgement. | A detached floating pupil would weaken the eye's anatomy. | Fixed eye corners; curved upper/lower lids. Clip the pupil with authored vector geometry as the aperture closes. | Open → close → hold → reopen. Light closes 55%; Strong closes fully with a readable hold. |
| Menu | Three bars become an X for the expanded state; a separate reverse restores the collapsed menu. | A repeated bar shuffle would not communicate navigation state. | Upper and lower bars meet at one center and rotate; middle bar retracts. The solid bars are closed capsules. | Collapsed bars ↔ expanded X. Light uses a direct 45-degree change; Strong opens the spacing by up to 2 units and resolves a 1.6-degree correction. |

Exact time samples, key poses, and rest markers are in definitions.mjs and the generated manifest. Review Download (rigid), Eye (flexible), and Menu (state change) first; then review the family together. No user approval gate is required before completing the batch.
